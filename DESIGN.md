## Brand & Style
The brand personality embodies refined Thai hospitality, contemplative stillness, and artisanal luxury. Drawing inspiration from secluded boutique wellness sanctuaries and high-end Thai retreats, the experience balances raw natural materiality—linen, weathered teak wood, and hand-cast earthenware—with restrained editorial minimalism. 

The aesthetic eschews generic spa tropes, playful illustrations, emojis, and visual clutter in favor of confident negative space, quiet asymmetry, and immaculate typographic hierarchy. The design style synthesizes high-end tactile minimalism with contemporary editorial elegance: large, breathing margins, subtle tonal shifts rather than heavy lines, and deliberate pacing that slows the user's focus into a state of unhurried calm.

## Colors
The palette evokes the sensory warmth of natural architectural materials found in luxury northern and southern Thai retreats:
- **Primary (`#2B1E16` - Deep Teak):** Used for primary typography, impactful branding accents, and primary action buttons. It provides authoritative, grounded contrast without the harsh, sterile chill of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#B86A4C` - Muted Terracotta):** Echoing sun-dried clay vessels and terracotta architectural tiles, this serves as an intentional focal accent for selected highlights, active states, and quiet notifications.
- **Tertiary (`#8C7A6B` - Herbal Clay / Muted Sandstone):** A nuanced intermediate tone for subtle metadata, divider accents, and inactive UI boundaries.
- **Neutral (`#F7F5F0` - Warm Ivory):** The canvas background, offering an organic, unbleached linen warmth that soothes the eye.
- **Linen Surface (`#EFECE6`):** Tonal surface for cards, input containers, and structural modules, creating quiet depth without elevation shadows.
- **Soft Charcoal (`#332F2C`):** Standard reading body color, maintaining WCAG AAA accessibility against warm ivory while preserving a softer, tactile presence.

## Typography
Typographic treatment follows a strict editorial cadence. Headlines honor Thai and Southeast Asian artisanal heritage through **Noto Serif** (matching aesthetic pairing with *Noto Serif Thai* or *Pridi* for Thai-script equivalents), delivering statuesque grace, open counters, and high cultural prestige. 

Body text and functional controls rely on the clean, disciplined geometry of **Inter** (pairing seamlessly with modern geometric Thai typefaces like *Sukhumvit*), ensuring flawless legibility across service durations, pricing, and treatment descriptions. Uppercase styling combined with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) is reserved exclusively for small subheadings, treatment categories, and navigation tags.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to an unhurried, rhythmically spacious 12-column grid on desktop (conforming to a max width of 1280px to preserve intimacy), transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

Generous macro-spacing (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) surrounds treatment narratives and booking panels, emulating the acoustic silence of a boutique treatment room. Sections never crowd one another; content blocks breathe naturally within generous outer margins (`4rem` on desktop, reducing gracefully to `1.25rem` on mobile screens).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed exclusively through **tonal stratification** and ultra-soft ambient occlusion, avoiding commercial dropshadows or floating synthetic glass.

- **Level 0 (Base):** Warm Ivory (`#F7F5F0`) canvas representing natural unbleached mulberry paper or raw linen.
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers):** Linen Surface (`#EFECE6`) layered flush or separated by hairline borders of Sand (`#D8D0C5`, 1px solid).
- **Level 2 (Active Overlays & Modals):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces lifted with a single, highly diffused botanical-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.06)`.
- **Dividers:** Hairline rules (`1px`) styled in Sand (`#E5DFD5`) strictly used to anchor editorial columns and booking summaries.

## Shapes
A disciplined **soft geometric structure (Level 1)** is adopted throughout all containers, buttons, and form inputs. Elements carry a gentle `0.25rem` (4px) curvature, softening sharp digital corners to echo hand-planed teak wood, stone slates, and architectural masonry. Circular shapes are strictly prohibited except for micro-indicators (e.g., date selection dots or custom radio discs).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in Deep Teak (`#2B1E16`), text in Warm Ivory (`#F7F5F0`). Padding: `14px 28px`. Typography: `label-lg` uppercase. Hover state deepens to `#1C140E` with a gentle transition (`200ms ease-out`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, hairline border (`1px solid #2B1E16`), text in Deep Teak. Hover fills subtly with `#2B1E16` at 5% opacity.
- **Tertiary Link:** Deep Teak text with an intentional underline offset by `6px`, transitioning to Muted Terracotta (`#B86A4C`) on interaction.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default State:** Background in Linen (`#EFECE6`), text in Charcoal (`#332F2C`), border in `#E5DFD5` (`1px`). Typography: `label-md`.
- **Selected State:** Background in Deep Teak (`#2B1E16`), text in Warm Ivory (`#F7F5F0`), border in Deep Teak.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Flat background in Linen (`#EFECE6`) or transparent with a bottom-only hairline border (`1px solid #D8D0C5`). Text in Soft Charcoal (`#332F2C`).
- **Focus State:** Solid underline or border transitions smoothly to Deep Teak (`#2B1E16`) with zero outer glow. Labels remain visible above in `label-md` tracking.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons:** Outer ring `18px` in `#8C7A6B`, active inner center filled with Deep Teak (`#2B1E16`) dot (`8px`).
- **Checkboxes:** Square with `2px` corner radius, hairline border in `#8C7A6B`, active fill in Deep Teak with a fine Warm Ivory checkmark mark.

### Cards (Treatment & Sanctuary Menus)
- **Menu Cards:** Grounded upon Linen Surface (`#EFECE6`) with flush padding (`32px`), zero drop-shadow. Contains treatment duration in `label-md` (Muted Terracotta), treatment name in `headline-sm` (Noto Serif), brief sensory narrative in `body-sm`, and right-aligned price in `body-md` (Deep Teak).
- **Interactive Hover:** Card border subtly transitions from invisible to `#D8D0C5` without vertical card displacement.

### Specialized Component: Treatment Time Slots
- Rectangular blocks (`48px` height) with soft `4px` borders, featuring clear time notation (`body-md`) and therapist availability. Inactive slots appear muted in `#D8D0C5` with a delicate line-through.
